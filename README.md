## 📌 What is SyllHost? — What does this project do?

SyllHost puts your domain and hosting in one place: search a domain, pick a hosting plan and go live — the hosting account is created instantly after payment and login details arrive by email. Customers manage domains, hosting, invoices and support from one client area, with clear renewal reminders and upgrades any time.

## 🎯 Who is it for?

Website owners, bloggers, businesses, agencies and resellers in Pakistan and worldwide who want fast hosting with instant setup and clear pricing.

## ⭐ Key Features & Services — What it offers

- ✅ Domain search & registration with upfront register / renew / transfer pricing
- ✅ Shared hosting plans with NVMe storage, free SSL and daily/hourly backups
- ✅ WordPress hosting and self-service reseller hosting lines
- ✅ Instant provisioning — account created automatically after payment
- ✅ cPanel control panel for files, email, databases and domains
- ✅ Transparent billing: PDF invoices, renewal reminders, auto-renew from balance
- ✅ Crypto and account-balance payments, support tickets & knowledge base
